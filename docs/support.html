Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen and enter your email address. You'll receive a password reset link within minutes.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > scroll to the bottom > tap "Delete Account". This will permanently delete your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > AgentRoute AI > Cancel Subscription.

How does offline mode work?

AgentRoute AI saves your work locally when you're offline. When you reconnect to the internet, your data automatically syncs to the cloud. Look for the sync indicator in the app to see pending uploads.

Can I use AgentRoute AI on multiple devices?

Yes! Sign in with the same account on any device. Your data syncs across all devices in real-time.

How do I join my team/agency?

Ask your Admin or Manager for an invitation link or token. Go to Settings > Account Mode > "Join Team / Connect to Upline" and enter the invitation details.

What happens to my data if I leave a team?

Your agent-owned records (leads, appointments, notes) remain with you and move to Solo mode. Your former team immediately loses access to your data.
