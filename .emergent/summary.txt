<original_problem_statement>
Build a production-ready iOS mobile SaaS app called AgentRoute AI for field sales agents.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (based on user requests during this and previous sessions):**
*   **Core App & Auth:** JWT-based auth, CRUD for Users, Leads, Appointments, and Scope of Appointment (SOA). A dashboard overview.
*   **Role-Based Login Routing:** After a user logs in, they should be routed to a specific dashboard based on their role (Admin, Manager, Agent).
*   **Lead Scanner (OCR):** A high-speed workflow to scan business cards, contact sheets, and handwritten notes to auto-populate lead fields with OCR, allow for review, and provide quick actions (Scan Next) after saving.
*   **AI Assistant & Daily Planner:** An OpenAI-based sales coach and a system to generate a prioritized daily action plan for agents.
*   **Agency Command Center:** A mobile-first admin dashboard providing a real-time view of agency performance, activity, and pipeline health with drill-down capabilities.
*   **Needs Attention & Coaching Alerts Dashboard:** A leadership dashboard that automatically identifies agents, leads, and cases that require intervention.
*   **Role-Based Hierarchy & Command Center:** Admin, Manager, and Agent roles with specific data visibility. A command center for managers/admins to view team activity and a visual team hierarchy tree.
*   **Policy Sales Pipeline:** A visual system to track leads through stages.
*   **Scope of Appointment (SOA) Workflow:** A professional, mobile-first fillable form with a native, finger-touch dual signature capture solution. It must generate a clean, official-looking PDF that can be saved, printed, or shared and is stored with the lead record.
*   **Commission Tracking:** A system to track estimated, pending, and paid commissions with split logic.
*   **Lead Distribution & Territory Management:** A system for admins/managers to assign leads manually or automatically based on territory rules.
*   **Bug Fix - Appointment Navigation:** Fix a bug where tapping an appointment in the Upcoming Appointments list leads to an Appointment not found screen.
*   **Future/Backlog Features:** Advanced route planner, production/commission tracking dashboards, agent productivity tools, training library, and App Store compliance pages.
</original_problem_statement>
