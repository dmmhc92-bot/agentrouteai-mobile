**Key Technical Concepts**
*   **Flexible Account Mode (Solo/Hierarchy)**: A system where a user's account identity is separate from their team membership, allowing them to join or leave hierarchies without creating a new account.
*   **Data Portability on Team Departure**: A critical business rule ensuring that when users leave a team, they retain ownership of their personal data, and the former team's access is revoked.
*   **Secure Role/Dashboard Routing**: Post-login, the app reads the user's role (, , ) and account mode from the backend and automatically routes them to the appropriate dashboard/UI. Self-promotion is prevented.
*   **Token-Based Invitation System**: Secure, unique tokens are generated by the backend for inviting users to a team. These tokens dictate the user's role and position in the hierarchy upon acceptance.
*   **Atomic Backend Operations**: Using database transactions (implicitly with single document updates, explicitly required for multi-document changes) to ensure that joining or leaving a team is an all-or-nothing operation, preventing inconsistent states.
*   **Targeted DB Debugging**: The process of directly connecting to the database via a script to inspect and repair corrupted or inconsistent data, which was necessary to fix the admin login issue.
